Privacy Policy for Roons Bot

Effective Date: 20 September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Roons Bot collects, uses, and protects your information. By using this bot on any Discord server, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

2. Data Collection

Roons Bot collects the following data to provide its functionality:

  • Guild ID: The unique identifier of the Discord server where the bot is used.
  • User ID: Your unique Discord identifier, as well as the ID of the user the bot is configured to follow when using /follow.
  • Voice Channel ID: The identifier of the voice channel where you interact with the bot or where the bot was active prior to a restart.
  • Volume Setting: Your preferred volume level for sound bites in the server.
  • Authentication Token ("Magic Number"): A unique randomly generated token generated upon request (via /magic-number) to authorize the AdmiralBulldog desktop application.
  • Feedback Messages: Any comments or suggestions voluntarily submitted via /feedback.

This data is collected automatically when you use the bot and is essential for delivering the bot's features.

3. Data Usage

The data collected by Roons Bot is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Ensuring the correct functioning of the bot and voice channel playback.
  • Providing users with a personalized experience (e.g., maintaining volume settings, following users across voice channels).
  • Authorizing playback requests from the desktop application via your authentication token.
  • Reviewing user feedback to improve the bot.

No personal information beyond the data mentioned is collected, and this data is not sold or shared with any third parties.

4. Data Retention

The data will be stored for as long as necessary to provide the bot’s services. When Roons Bot leaves or is removed from a Discord server, all guild settings and associated authentication tokens for that server are automatically purged. If you wish to have your data deleted manually, please contact the bot's developer, and your data will be removed upon request.

5. Data Security

We take the protection of your data seriously. Appropriate measures are in place to prevent unauthorized access, use, or disclosure of your information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the data stored about you.
  • Request the correction or deletion of your data.
  • Withdraw your consent to data collection (though this may limit your ability to use the bot).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact mrbean355@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. You are encouraged to review this policy regularly. Your continued use of the bot signifies your acceptance of any changes.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact mrbean355@gmail.com.
